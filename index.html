    Good To Know

    Common Surveying Questions

    Straight answers to the questions Palm Coast and Flagler County property owners ask us most.

    Do I need a survey to build a fence in Palm Coast?+

    In most cases, yes. A fence built over a property line or into an easement can lead to disputes, fines, or being required to move the fence at your cost. A boundary survey marks your exact property lines and corners so your fence goes in the right place the first time. Many fence companies in Palm Coast ask for a survey before they start, and a survey also helps with permits. Stephenson Wilcox & Associates can mark your lines clearly so your fence company can build with confidence.

    What is an elevation certificate?+

    An elevation certificate is an official FEMA document prepared by a licensed surveyor. It records how high your home sits compared to the expected flood level for your property, called the base flood elevation. Insurance companies use it to set your flood insurance premium, and lenders may require it before closing. If your home sits higher than the flood level, the certificate can help lower your premium. Stephenson Wilcox & Associates prepares elevation certificates throughout Palm Coast and Flagler County.

    How long does a boundary survey take?+

    Most residential boundary surveys in Flagler County are completed within one to two weeks from the day you order, depending on our schedule, the size of the property, and how much research the parcel requires. Very wooded lots or older properties with missing records can take a little longer. When you call, we will give you a clear time estimate up front and keep you updated. If you have a closing deadline, tell us right away so we can plan around it.

    How much does a land survey cost in Flagler County?+

    The cost of a survey depends on the type of survey, the size and shape of the property, vegetation, and how complete the existing records are. A small, clear residential lot costs less than a large or heavily wooded parcel. Because every property is different, we quote each job individually so you only pay for the work your land actually needs. Call Stephenson Wilcox & Associates at (386) 437-2363 and we will give you a straightforward price for your property with no obligation.

    What is a boundary survey, and when do I need one?+

    A boundary survey establishes the exact property lines and corners of your land, based on your deed, recorded plats, and markers found in the field. You typically need one before building a fence, pool, or addition, when buying or selling property, when settling a line dispute with a neighbor, or when splitting land. The surveyor sets or flags your corners and provides a signed survey drawing you can rely on for permits, closings, and construction.

    What is an ALTA/NSPS land title survey?+

    An ALTA/NSPS land title survey is a detailed survey prepared to national standards set by the American Land Title Association and the National Society of Professional Surveyors. It is usually ordered for commercial property purchases, larger land deals, and lender or title company requirements. It shows boundaries, improvements, easements, encroachments, and access in far more detail than a standard survey. Stephenson Wilcox & Associates prepares ALTA/NSPS surveys for attorneys, lenders, and title companies across Flagler County.

    What is a topographic survey used for?+

    A topographic survey maps the shape of the land: elevations, slopes, drainage patterns, trees, and existing features such as buildings, driveways, and utilities. Architects and engineers use it to design homes, sites, septic systems, and drainage that work with the land instead of against it. In low-lying parts of Flagler County, good elevation data also helps with flood planning and permitting. The result is an accurate map your design team can build from.

    What is construction staking?+

    Construction staking transfers the design from paper plans onto the ground. Our field crews place stakes and marks that show builders exactly where to put foundations, walls, driveways, utilities, and roads, at the correct position and elevation. Accurate staking prevents expensive mistakes, failed inspections, and rebuilds. Stephenson Wilcox & Associates provides construction staking for home builders, site contractors, and developers throughout Palm Coast and Flagler County, from single homes to full subdivisions.

    My property corner markers are missing. Can you find them?+

    Yes. Missing or buried corner markers are very common in Flagler County, especially on older lots. Using your deed, the recorded plat, and markers on neighboring properties, we retrace your boundaries and reset your corners with new, durable markers. We then flag them clearly so you, your fence company, or your builder can see exactly where your lines are. A boundary survey also gives you a signed drawing that documents the corners for your records.
